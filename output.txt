  THEORETICAL FOUNDATION (THEOREM 2 — RIEMANNIAN ROBBINS-SIEGMUND ALMOST-SURE CONVERGENCE):
  Let M = (S^{p-1})^V be the compact product hypersphere. Under:
    (A1) C^1 smoothness and lower-bounded loss L(Theta) >= 0 with L_g-Lipschitz Riemannian gradient;
    (A2) First-order metric retraction R_Theta(xi) = (Theta + xi)/||Theta + xi||_2 with ||R(xi) - (Theta+xi)|| <= C ||xi||^2;
    (A3) Unbiased mini-batch gradients E[g_t | F_t] = grad L(Theta_t) with bounded variance E[||g_t||^2 | F_t] <= sigma^2 + M ||grad L||^2;
    (A4) Robbins-Monro step-size schedule: sum eta_t = oo and sum eta_t^2 < oo;
  Bonnabel's Riemannian Robbins-Siegmund Theorem (IEEE TAC 2013) guarantees almost-sure convergence to the
  Riemannian stationary critical set: lim_{t->oo} ||grad L(Theta_t)||_2 = 0 (almost surely).
  [Note: Standard in non-convex optimization, Theorem 2 guarantees convergence to stationary points, not global minima.]
